Read coverage alongside counts

Missing, empty and failed are different.

Successful empty

A valid resource observation can explicitly report no returned records.

Failed or uncollected

Unknown, not zero. Check source outcomes, enabled modules and authorization.

Legacy observations

A folder date may not be an exact collection time. Retained files do not establish a complete current snapshot.

Notebook execution can succeed while reporting incomplete, invalid or stale collection.

A controlled experiment, not routine collection

Capture. Rerun unchanged writers. Compare.

1 / Capture

Keep collectors and other writers idle. VALIDATION_ACTION = "capture" saves a new metadata baseline outside raw evidence.

2 / Rerun

Same evidence, settings and scope. Rerun selected transformation writers serially with publication enabled.

3 / Compare

VALIDATION_ACTION = "compare" uses the same baseline and retained Delta versions to check exact curated contents.

Baselines are never overwritten. Retain Delta history; do not vacuum or change retention during the experiment.

Interpret validation precisely

A rerun pass has a specific meaning.

PASS

Exact rerun stability for the unchanged-input experiment. Not proof of freshness or collection completeness.

NOT_COMPARABLE

Evidence, settings or selected scope changed. Not a rerun pass or failure.

UNVERIFIED

Required historic Delta data is unavailable. Do not reinterpret missing evidence as a pass.

Investigate an actual same-input content mismatch. Do not rewrite evidence or baselines to conceal it.
