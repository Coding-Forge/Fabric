Welcome to Fabric Monitor. This series follows the journey from authorized collection to a model-ready data foundation.

You can explore the solution locally on Windows or Linux. Windows users can work directly in PowerShell, or choose WSL2 for the best of both worlds: a Windows desktop with a Linux development environment.

For production, we will compare an Azure Function App with notebook-based collection and orchestration. Then we will follow the retained evidence into curated Delta tables.

Throughout the series, we will distinguish successful execution from complete collection. Missing evidence is not zero, and historical observations are not automatically current state.
